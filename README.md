## 📚 References, Data Sources & Literature

To strengthen the scientific foundation of our project and support our simulation models (DNA damage, neurological, cardiovascular, etc.), we utilized the following official NASA datasets, test data, and academic articles:

### 🛰️ Original NASA Data Sources
- **[NASA Life Sciences Data Archive (LSAH)](https://nlsp.nasa.gov/resources/lsah)** — Human spaceflight medicine and life sciences archives.
- **[NASA OSDR - SpaceX Inspiration4 Mission](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/missions/SpaceX%20Inspiration4)** — Biological and physiological datasets from the Inspiration4 mission.
- **[NASA OSDR - Open Science Repository Search](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/search?q=&data_source=cgene,alsda,esa&data_type=study)** — Genomic and space biology study search portal.
- **[NASA PSI Physical Sciences Repository](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/search?q=&data_source=psi&data_type=investigation)** — Physical sciences and space research database.

### 🧪 Test Data & Simulation References
- **[WESAdviz Interactive Test Data](https://kristofvl.github.io/wesadviz/index.html?sbj=6)** — Reference dataset for project testing and visualization scenarios.

### 📄 Scientific Articles & Literature
- **[PMC Article (PMC7198903)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7198903/)** — Academic review on spaceflight physiology and health risks.
- **[Frontiers in Physiology Study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2020.00394/full)** — Effects of space environment on human biology.
- **[ESA Mars500 Study Overview](https://www.esa.int/articles/mars500-study-overview)** — Long-term isolation and human health simulation project (ESA).
- **[Nature Article (s41586-024-07648-x)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07648-x)** — Current research on the genetic and molecular impacts of spaceflight.
